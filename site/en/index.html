What is Althea?

Our philosophy

Althea refers to wholeness and healing in Greek. For us, it means reconnecting with what is most authentic within us, beneath the experiences, beliefs, expectations and coping strategies we have accumulated throughout our lives. At Althea, we are interested in what will emerge in a psychedelic experience when a person is truly seen and held. In a safe, contained space, something wiser than the mind can begin to speak.

The different layers of the experience

Explore the core of who you are.

A guided psilocybin experience invites you to pause and reflect on your life, exploring what is happening within you beneath familiar roles, expectations and patterns of behavior.

A fresh perspective on yourself beyond familiar patterns

The experience helps you see your beliefs, reactions and patterns of behavior from a new perspective, and better understand their origins and influence on your life. Meeting your habits, fears and defences with greater understanding can bring more compassion to your relationship with yourself.

You may also gain distance from recurring patterns, whether they involve lifestyle, substances or relationships, making room for new choices, creativity and possibilities.

The experience helps you recognize what truly matters in your life and which direction feels authentically your own.

Connection with your body and emotions

The experience deepens your connection with your body, your emotions and different parts of your psyche. It offers an opportunity to meet and open up to deeper parts of yourself that may have been with you since childhood. Bringing them into awareness and meeting them with compassion can soften old tension, bring understanding to long-held patterns, and allow them to be integrated.

The aim is not to get rid of uncomfortable or difficult feelings and experiences, but to give them space to be met in a safe environment.
Feeling these emotions allows the energy held beneath them to be released, creating room for deeper inner peace and healing.

Your relationship with yourself and others

The experience opens up new perspectives on how you relate to yourself and others. It creates an opportunity to explore your needs, boundaries and ways of seeking connection or protecting yourself, while strengthening the inner resources that help you be more freely yourself.

As a couple, you can also explore recurring situations and patterns of interaction in your relationship, and what might strengthen the connection, safety and wellbeing between you. The aim is to deepen your understanding of both your own experience and your partner’s, and find more open and authentic ways of relating to one another.

Profound meaning and spiritual experience

A psilocybin experience can involve a deep sense of connection: with yourself, loved ones, nature or life as a whole. Some people describe deep gratitude, acceptance or a profound feeling of being part of something greater than themselves.

A spiritual experience can also be difficult to put into words. Experiences like these can help clarify your values and what matters most to you in life. Their meaning is explored on your own terms, not imposed from outside.

Life after the experience

In the first days and weeks, some people describe greater openness, lightness or connection with themselves and others. Daily activities, such as meeting loved ones or spending time in nature, may feel more meaningful than before. This period after the experience is sometimes called the afterglow.

Research has found increased cognitive flexibility after psilocybin use. The period following the experience is sometimes described as a possible window of neuroplasticity: a time when learning new things and reflecting on familiar patterns may become easier.

In everyday life, this can mean having more space between a stimulus and your response. You may notice a familiar impulse to withdraw, defend yourself or please others, yet find that you can pause before acting on it. Alongside the automatic reaction, another way of responding becomes possible: one that supports your own values and needs.

One program · three phases

An experience that begins before the journey.

You get to know us before the experience. We are present with you throughout the experience and support you during the integration process.

01

Two preparation sessions

Preparation

We get to know your life situation and what you hope to gain from the process.

  • Together, we review your health information, background and key safety factors to assess whether the experience is suitable for you.
  • We explore possible intentions, without expecting you to have all the answers.
  • You'll receive practical guidance for the experience and your journey.
02

Experience day · approx. 6–8 hours

Guided experience

The experience takes place in a private setting close to nature.

  • We begin with a guided meditation and a somatic exercise to help your nervous system and body settle into the experience.
  • We carefully discuss the use of high-quality psilocybin-containing truffles and the dosage with you beforehand.
  • At the end of the day, we take time to reflect on your experience and the themes it has brought up.
03

Two sessions · over 2–6 weeks

Integration

Integration gives space to reflect on what emerged and what it means for you.

  • The integration process begins at the end of the experience day.
  • You continue with the same facilitators in two sessions after returning home.
  • We help you make sense of your experience and deepen the insights it has brought. Together, we explore how to bring these into your everyday life through practical choices, habits and changes.

What would you like to know?

Click on the question to read the answer. You are welcome to bring these or any other questions to your introductory call.

Who is the program not suitable for?

The experience is not suitable if you have a psychotic disorder (or a family history of one), bipolar disorder or a significant cardiovascular condition, certain medications or if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. Any medication you use is assessed individually before the session.

Do not change your medication on your own in order to take part in the programme. We discuss your circumstances before you decide whether to participate and refer you for a professional assessment where needed.

What if I feel anxious during the experience?

The experience may bring up strong feelings or difficult material, while also opening up new perspectives, deepening self-understanding and strengthening resilience. You will not be alone: the facilitators are present throughout the experience, and integration offers time to make sense of it in the context of your life. At its best, even a challenging experience can open up a meaningful opportunity for growth and change.

Why does the program take place in the Netherlands?

Truffles containing psilocybin are legally available in the Netherlands. They are used entirely there and are not brought into Finland or other countries.

Information on Dutch law · Trimbos

What is used in the psilocybin experience?

We use psilocybin-containing truffles, the underground sclerotia of the fungus. Psilocybin can alter perception, emotions and cognition. Experiences and their effects vary from person to person.

How is the dose decided?

We discuss the dose with you before the experience. During preparation, we go through your wishes and any questions about how the program will be carried out.

How long does the experience last?

We allow approximately 6–8 hours for the guided experience. The duration and course of the effects vary from person to person.

When can I travel home?

We recommend booking your return flight no earlier than the day after the programme ends. This leaves time for meaningful integration after the experience.

What does the research say?

Psilocybin is being studied in relation to conditions such as depression. These studies involve treatment programmes that include professional support. Research findings do not guarantee a particular outcome for any individual participant. Althea offers individual and two-person facilitation, not a clinical trial or healthcare treatment.
